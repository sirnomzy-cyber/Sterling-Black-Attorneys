Careers at Sterling & Black

Build a career grounded in mentorship and meaningful work

We invest in attorneys and staff who want to do serious legal work without losing sight of who they're doing it for.

Why Join Us

What sets our culture apart

Structured Mentorship

Every associate is paired with a partner mentor from day one, with regular check-ins built into the review cycle.

Real Client Exposure

Associates work directly with clients early - not just document review in the background.

Professional Development

Continuing education support, conference attendance, and clear paths to partnership.

Collaborative Practice

Attorneys across practice areas work together rather than in isolated silos.

Community Commitment

Paid time for pro bono work and community legal education initiatives.

Competitive Benefits

Comprehensive health coverage, retirement matching, and generous parental leave.
