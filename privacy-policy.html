Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Sterling & Black Attorneys LLP ("the Firm," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email, phone number, and details submitted through our contact and consultation forms. We also collect limited technical information automatically, such as browser type and pages visited, to help us improve the site.

How We Use Information

We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, schedule consultations, and - where you've opted in - send legal insights and firm updates. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Attorney-Client Relationship

Submitting information through this website does not, by itself, create an attorney-client relationship. Please avoid sending confidential information through general contact forms until an engagement has been formally established.

Cookies

This site may use cookies to support basic functionality and understand aggregate usage patterns. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Your Choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time using the link included in those messages, or by contacting us directly.
