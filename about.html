    About Sterling & Black

    Trusted legal counsel built on experience and integrity

    A full-service firm founded on the belief that exceptional legal representation combines deep legal knowledge with genuine client relationships.

    Our Story

    Nearly three decades of strategic counsel

    Sterling & Black Attorneys was founded on the belief that exceptional legal representation combines deep legal knowledge with genuine client relationships. What began as a small commercial practice has grown into a full-service firm serving individuals, businesses, investors, and institutions nationwide.

    Every matter receives personalized attention, practical legal strategies, and a commitment to achieving the best possible outcome within the framework of the law. As the firm has grown, that founding philosophy — direct attorney access, clear communication, and strategic thinking — has remained unchanged.

    Our Mission

    To provide trusted legal counsel through strategic thinking, ethical practice, exceptional advocacy, and lasting client relationships.

    Our Vision

    To be one of the most respected full-service law firms, recognized for legal excellence, innovation, integrity, and unwavering client service.

    Our History

    Milestones along the way

    1998

    Sterling & Black founded as a three-attorney commercial practice in Lower Manhattan.

    2004

    Retained as outside counsel for the firm's first Fortune 500 corporate client.

    2009

    Expanded into family law, real estate, and estate planning practice areas.

    2014

    Opened a second office in Chicago to serve growing Midwest client base.

    2019

    Recognized with the firm's first Regional Law Firm of the Year distinction.

    2023

    Launched a formal pro bono partnership with regional legal aid clinics.

    2026

    Celebrating over 27 years of continuous practice and a third office in San Francisco.

    Firm Culture

    A collaborative, ethical practice

    Attorneys across practice areas work together on complex matters, supported by structured mentorship, continuous professional development, and a firm-wide commitment to ethical decision-making and inclusion.
