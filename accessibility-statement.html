Accessibility Statement

Last updated: January 2026

Sterling & Black Attorneys LLP is committed to ensuring this website is accessible to all visitors, including individuals with disabilities. We work to meet or exceed the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

Accessibility Features

  • Keyboard-accessible navigation, menus, and forms
  • Visible focus indicators throughout the site
  • Descriptive alt text for meaningful images
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background
  • Support for reduced-motion preferences
  • A skip-to-content link on every page

Ongoing Efforts

Accessibility is an ongoing process. We periodically review this site and welcome feedback that helps us identify and address barriers.

Feedback

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this website, please contact us at accessibility@sterlingblacklaw.com or (212) 555-0142, and we will work to address the issue promptly.
