    Before You Meet With Us

    What to expect from your consultation

    A confidential consultation is a conversation, not a commitment. Here's exactly how it works, from your first request to your next steps.

    Schedule a Consultation

    Before

    Requesting your consultation

    You'll start by submitting a short consultation request - your contact information, the practice area involved, and a brief description of your situation. This takes about three minutes and does not create an attorney-client relationship.

    A member of our team reviews every request personally to match you with the right attorney based on your practice area and, where relevant, any attorney preference you note.

    Typical response time

    Within one business day, by phone or email, to confirm your appointment and consultation method.

    During

    Your first conversation with an attorney

    30–45 minutes

    Enough time to walk through your situation without feeling rushed, and to ask questions of your own.

    In-person, virtual, or phone

    Choose whichever format is most convenient - the consultation itself doesn't change based on method.

    Fully confidential

    Everything discussed is protected, whether or not you decide to move forward with representation.

    After

    Deciding on next steps

    By the end of the conversation, your attorney will outline the realistic options available to you, an estimated scope of work, and - if you choose to proceed - a written engagement agreement covering fees and next steps. There's no pressure to decide immediately, and no obligation if you'd like time to think it over.

    Preparation Checklist

    How to make the most of your time

    • A brief written timeline of the events relevant to your matter
    • Copies of any contracts, notices, or correspondence involved
    • A list of the questions you most want answered
    • Your general goals - what a good outcome would look like for you
    • Names of any other parties or counsel already involved, if applicable

    Ready when you are

    Requesting a consultation takes a few minutes and carries no obligation.
