    Corporate & Business Law

    From formation through growth-stage transactions, we provide the legal infrastructure businesses need to move quickly and with confidence.

    Schedule Consultation Read FAQs

    Overview

    Our corporate and business law team advises companies at every stage — from initial formation through fundraising, growth, and eventual sale or succession. We serve as embedded legal counsel for founders and executives who need practical, business-minded advice, not just contract review.

    Services include business formation, corporate governance, shareholder and partnership agreements, mergers and acquisitions, corporate restructuring, commercial contracts, business advisory, regulatory compliance, and corporate secretarial services.

    Who We Represent

    • Founders and early-stage startups
    • Privately held and family-owned businesses
    • Private equity and venture-backed companies
    • Mid-market and multinational corporations
    • Boards of directors and executive leadership

    Common Legal Challenges

    • Structuring a business entity for tax and liability protection
    • Negotiating founder, investor, and shareholder agreements
    • Navigating the legal complexity of a merger or acquisition
    • Keeping pace with regulatory and compliance obligations
    • Drafting commercial contracts that protect the business long-term

    Our Approach

    We start by understanding your business objectives, not just the immediate legal question. That context shapes every recommendation — from how we structure an entity to how we negotiate a term sheet — so legal decisions support where the business is headed, not just where it is today.
