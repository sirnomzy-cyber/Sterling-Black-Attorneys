    Managing Partner

    Margaret Ellison Sterling

    Corporate & Business Law · 29 Years of Practice

    Biography

    Margaret co-founded Sterling & Black in 1998 and has led the firm's corporate practice ever since. She advises founders, boards, and executive teams on formation, governance, financing, and mergers and acquisitions, with particular depth in middle-market transactions.

    Clients describe her approach as direct and commercially minded — she is known for translating complex deal structures into decisions a board can act on quickly. Margaret also chairs the firm's mentorship program for early-career attorneys.
